# Known limitations

- The preview runs trusted local apps on Windows. App-addressed Remote and independent processes provide an application boundary. OS sandboxing, hostile same-user isolation, unrestricted descendant containment and production dispatcher activation remain `NOT_VERIFIED`.
- Native features depend on the exact guest and Makepad Remote pair. RudraOffice, AI CAD, the DWG worker and Codex are external. The installed Windows smoke covered one admitted external RudraOffice with a read attachment. The control, resize and two-app scenarios have separate development-bundle evidence. [Verification scope](TESTING.md)
- Desktop includes fixed RudraOffice and AI CAD launch tabs plus a shared-app attachment view. Human View resize and input are limited to an owned or control-shared RudraOffice instance. AI CAD and read attachments are view-only in Desktop. A distinct owner connection can grant or revoke sharing. [Configuration](CONFIGURATION.md#4-use-ai-direct-and-human-view-on-the-same-instance)
- Recording handoff was demonstrated on a development bundle with an authenticated Codex CLI. The reviewed package analysis and live AI Direct are separate flows. Preserve `OUTCOME_UNKNOWN` if a dispatched action or handoff cannot be verified. [Handoff configuration](CONFIGURATION.md#5-record-and-review-a-demonstration)
- Keep `MSS_DATA_ROOT` short. With the same preview Desktop and RudraOffice EXEs on one host, a 71-character data root qualified Remote, while a 111-character root produced a 273-character child `TEMP/TMP` directory and failed discovery in the expected directory. In an isolated diagnostic launcher, `TEMP/TMP` of length 248 and discovery of length 274 worked, while `TEMP/TMP` of length 272 failed discovery. A long `session_root` with short `TEMP/TMP` worked. The fallback discovery location and exact system threshold are `NOT_VERIFIED`. A separate 281-character guest EXE path failed at process creation before a PID could be recorded. [Architecture and launcher flow](doc/architecture.md)
- The local RC2 MSI contains external-runtime callers and no files for the nine fonts in the EXE's font manifest. Its same-host smoke and the standalone Python/PowerShell candidate smoke leave clean-host Makepad resource availability `NOT_RUN`. Public binary distribution is `BLOCKED` pending portable resources and a complete redistribution review and notice set. [Windows MSI notes](packaging/windows/README.md)
